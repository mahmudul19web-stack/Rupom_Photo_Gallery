YOUR PHOTO FOLDER

Put your own photos in this folder. Example:
- travel-sajek.jpg
- bike-ride.jpg
- nature-view.jpg

Then update the image src in index.html, for example:
<img src="Images/travel-sajek.jpg" alt="My Sajek trip" loading="lazy">

The demo cards currently use online Unsplash images. Replace their src values with your own Images/filename paths.
